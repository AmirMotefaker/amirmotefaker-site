# Content Growth v9 — Execution Plan

This release is focused on content depth and authority, not visual redesign.

## Delivery order

1. Canonical product integrity — Tasvia and active product routing.
2. Product page depth — problem, audience, capabilities, solution, lifecycle, roadmap and evidence.
3. Founder authority — stronger links between founder narrative and products.
4. Internal linking — products, Technology News and founder pages.
5. SEO/AI discoverability — canonical metadata, structured data and sitemap verification.
6. Desktop/mobile QA, CI, Preview, review and release.

## Guardrails

- No invented traction, customers, revenue, launch dates or public domains.
- No broad redesign of the approved v8 visual system.
- Persian remains Vazirmatn; English remains Space Grotesk.
- Production stays unchanged until review gates pass.
