## Purpose

Provide implementation-ready Persian and English copy for the AmirMotefaker.ir homepage while preserving the founder-led portfolio narrative, evidence discipline and canonical product state rules.

This deck is a copy source, not authorization to publish unsupported claims.

## Voice principles

- Builder/operator language over celebrity-founder language.
- Specific nouns over abstract adjectives.
- Products and evidence over self-praise.
- Current truth before future ambition.
- Persian should read naturally rather than as a literal translation of English.
- English must work as a complete international evaluation surface.

## 2. Thesis preview

### Persian

**Eyebrow**

`نگاه من به ساخت محصول`

**Heading**

`محصول خوب از فناوری شروع نمی‌شود؛ از یک مسئله واقعی شروع می‌شود.`

**Body**

`در پروژه‌هایی که می‌سازم، فناوری زمانی ارزشمند است که اصطکاک را کم کند، تصمیم‌گیری را بهتر کند یا یک تجربه پیچیده را ساده‌تر سازد. هوش مصنوعی برای من یک برچسب بازاریابی نیست؛ یکی از ابزارهای ساختن محصول بهتر است.`

**Supporting principles**

1. `مسئله قبل از تکنولوژی` — ابتدا مسئله و رفتار واقعی کاربر را می‌فهمیم.
2. `محصول قبل از نمایش` — قابلیت واقعی مهم‌تر از ادعای بزرگ است.
3. `سیستم قبل از ویژگی` — محصول باید بتواند رشد کند، یاد بگیرد و به اکوسیستم متصل شود.

**CTA**

`مطالعه کامل Thesis`

### English

**Eyebrow**

`PRODUCT THESIS`

**Heading**

`Good products don't start with technology. They start with a real problem.`

**Body**

`Across the products I build, technology matters when it reduces friction, improves decisions or makes a complex experience simpler. AI is not the proposition by itself; it is one of the tools for building a better product.`

**Supporting principles**

1. `Problem before technology` — understand the real user problem and behavior first.
2. `Product before presentation` — working capability matters more than oversized claims.

## 12. Implementation mapping

The homepage implementation should source:

- founder identity and verified career claims from founder canonical content;
- active product membership and names from `brand-registry.ts`;
- product descriptions and lifecycle from `product-portfolio.ts` after state reconciliation;
- Notes from the new Notes content model, not legacy WordPress news;
- proof items from an explicit evidence registry rather than arbitrary metrics in components.

No component should recreate product names, ordering or lifecycle mappings locally.

## Acceptance gate before implementation

1. Active portfolio membership matches the canonical registry.
2. Product one-line truths do not overstate current lifecycle.
3. Hero contains no unsupported superlatives or metrics.
4. Proof section renders only evidence-backed artifacts.
5. Persian and English copy are both complete.
6. Notes are distinguished from legacy News.
7. Navigation matches IA v7.0.
8. Product counts, if displayed, are computed rather than manually written.
9. Pending-classification assets do not appear as confirmed active portfolio members.
10. Production remains untouched.
