## Product-page standard
Hero, official-site CTA, positioning, problem, solution, audience, capabilities, differentiators, current state, founder role, verified technology, progress/release timeline, roadmap, visuals, FAQ, related products and final CTA.

## Design principles
- strong visual hierarchy
- restrained palette
- bilingual typography tuned separately for FA/EN
- bento portfolio composition
- subtle depth/borders rather than heavy glass effects
- real dark mode
- visible keyboard focus
- reduced-motion support
- responsive at 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440
- no generic AI purple/pink gradient aesthetic

## SEO / GEO
Canonical and hreflang, product-specific metadata, SoftwareApplication/WebPage/Breadcrumb schema, visible FAQ + FAQ schema where appropriate, sameAs to official domains, entity graph, semantic headings, answer-first copy, topic clusters, internal linking, sitemap/robots/feed validation.

## Safety
Production and DNS remain untouched until preview, CI, visual QA and SEO/GEO QA pass.
